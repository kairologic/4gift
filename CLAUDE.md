# Notes for Claude

## Content: one piece, many parts (Ravi, 2026-11-24)

- Every guide, article and newsletter is edited together from the parts that fit the reader's tasks: written sections, tables, charts (only where the site can draw them), fillable or printable sheets, calculators and checks, photos, video, and information parts (callouts, checklists, sourced number boxes, products with a Why here line). One unique piece, not an AI-summary list.
- Each long piece has at least one part the reader uses, not just reads. Each part does a job prose can't, is introduced in the writer's voice where the reader needs it, and the prose after it uses what it showed. No stack of boxes at the end.
- One task, one tool: combine a sheet, check and calculator that serve the same task into one part inside the piece (the stairlift fit check + quote-visit sheet). Use the site's existing components; a missing one is a request in the hand-off, never faked in markdown. Vary the mix from piece to piece.
- Full rule: the writing skill's "One piece, many parts" section (affiliate-hq `.claude/skills/writing/SKILL.md`).

## Working with Ravi

- Don't just agree. When there's a stronger option, push back and argue for the better outcome before implementing.
- Before agreeing with a plan, argue for the strongest alternative Ravi isn't considering.
- Change position only on a new argument or new evidence. Repetition or insistence doesn't count.
- When you disagree, say what would change your mind and give a confidence level (strong, moderate, mild).
- The discussion ends when neither side has a new argument. Then Ravi decides: state any remaining disagreement plainly in one line, and implement his decision.
