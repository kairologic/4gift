<!-- visual: callout | Short list: 4 groups of housewarming gifts, plus what to skip -->

For a friend's new home, a housewarming party or a thank-you to the host, the best gift is easy to carry in, easy to use and easy to find a spot for. Here's the short list:

- **For the host tonight:** a scented soy candle, a gourmet snack box or a set of stemless wine glasses. Things they can enjoy without finding a shelf.
- **For the new kitchen:** a personalized cutting board, an enameled Dutch oven or an indoor herb garden kit for the windowsill.
- **For the new walls:** a scratch-off world map or a Wi-Fi photo frame the family can fill.
- **For a home with pets:** a houseplant the ASPCA lists as non-toxic, like a spider plant or a parlor palm.
- **Skip** anything big, anything that has to match their decor, and lilies if they have a cat.

## What makes a good housewarming gift?

<!-- visual: table | 6 gifts with best for, why it works and skip it if, after 3 questions -->

Something that helps a home feel lived in, without taking up the space they just cleared. Small and useful beats big and showy.

Ask yourself three things before you buy:

1. **Is it a host gift or a home gift?** At a party, the host is busy. A candle or a snack box can wait; a dish that needs serving can't.
2. **Will it fit their style?** If you don't know, choose something for the kitchen or something they'll use up.
3. **Can they carry it in?** A new home is already full of boxes.

| Gift | Best for | Why it works | Skip it if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soy candle | Any host | Easy, warm, used up | They have small kids or open-flame rules |
| Snack box | Party hosts | Shared on the night | Allergies in the house |
| Cutting board, engraved | Couples, first homes | Used daily, stays out | You're unsure of names or spelling |
| Herb garden kit | Cooks with a sunny sill | Fresh herbs on the sill | No window light |
| Pet-safe houseplant | Homes with dogs or cats | Lives for years | They have no light at all |
| Wi-Fi photo frame | Families far apart | Keeps filling with photos | No Wi-Fi set up yet |

## Which houseplants are safe for a home with pets?

<!-- visual: table | ASPCA list: 2 non-toxic plants vs 3 toxic ones for dogs and cats -->

Choose one the ASPCA lists as non-toxic. Its plant database lists the spider plant and the parlor palm as non-toxic to dogs and cats.

Two popular gift plants are on the other side of the list. The ASPCA lists golden pothos and the snake plant as toxic to dogs and cats. Pothos can cause mouth irritation, drooling and vomiting, and snake plant can cause nausea, vomiting and diarrhea, per the ASPCA.

> **Skip lilies for a cat household.** The ASPCA lists the Easter lily as toxic to cats, with signs that include vomiting, lethargy and kidney failure, and says death is possible. Cats are the only species it lists as affected.

Of the 5 plants above, the ASPCA lists 2 as safe for dogs and cats and 3 as toxic to at least one. If you don't know whether they have pets, a spider plant is the safe choice.

## What's a good gift for someone who just moved in?

Something for the kitchen. It's the room every new home uses on day one, and kitchen gifts rarely clash with anyone's style.

An engraved cutting board with their names or move-in date is personal and practical. An enameled Dutch oven covers soups, stews and bread for years. An indoor herb garden kit gives them basil and mint on the windowsill. All three fit into a kitchen without needing a decision about decor.

## What can you bring to a housewarming party?

<!-- visual: callout | 3 easy host gifts to hand over at the door -->

Something the host doesn't have to deal with on the night. Hand it over, and let them enjoy it later.

- **A candle:** a soy candle is the classic host gift, and it's easy to pass along if it isn't their scent.
- **A snack box:** set it out for guests, or save it for the week after the move when nobody wants to cook.
- **Wine glasses:** a set of stemless glasses suits a first home, where half the glasses are still in a box somewhere.

Add a short card with your name. After a party, hosts often lose track of who brought what.

## What about a family that lives far from their relatives?

A Wi-Fi photo frame, so grandparents and cousins can keep sending photos to the new place. It turns an empty shelf into a spot that changes every week.

PhotoWorkout says Skylight lets family email photos to the frame's own address, and Aura's site says its frames need no subscription. Skylight also says photos already on the frame keep showing without Wi-Fi, which helps in a new home where the internet isn't set up yet. NBC Select lists 16 GB of storage built into the Skylight Frame 2, and Skylight lists a 3-year limited warranty. Load 20 or 30 photos before you wrap it, so the frame isn't empty on day one. ## What should you avoid?

<!-- visual: callout | 4 kinds of housewarming gifts to skip -->

Anything that makes work or asks for space. A new home comes with plenty of both.

- **Big decor:** art, rugs and furniture are personal choices. Leave them to the owners.
- **Fragile pieces:** a crystal vase is one more thing to unpack and worry about.
- **Toxic plants for a pet home:** the ASPCA lists golden pothos, snake plants and Easter lilies among them.
- **Gag gifts:** a joke about the mortgage lands differently with someone who just signed one.

## So what would I give?

A pet-safe plant like a spider plant for a home gift, and a soy candle or a snack box for the night of the party.

The plant lasts for years and is safe if a dog or cat moves in later, according to the ASPCA's list. The candle or snack box is easy to hand over at the door. For a family far from relatives, a photo frame is the gift they'll still be looking at next year.
