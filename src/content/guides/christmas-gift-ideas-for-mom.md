<!-- visual: callout | Short list: 4 kinds of mom and a gift for each, plus 1 skip -->

For mom at Christmas, the gifts that land are the ones that give her comfort, time to herself or a little more of the family. Here's the short list:

- **For the mom far from family:** a Wi-Fi photo frame everyone can send photos to. NBC Select preferred the Aura, and PhotoWorkout calls Skylight the easiest for less tech-savvy users.
- **For the mom who's always cold on the couch:** a weighted blanket. Mattress Clarity says the beaded Gravity Blanket suits people who get cold quickly.
- **For the mom who reads in bed:** an e-reader, or a rechargeable book light if she prefers paper.
- **For the mom whose tea goes cold:** a heated mug. Best Buy says the Ember Mug 2 holds a set temperature for up to 1.5 hours off its coaster.
- **Skip** anything that's really a gift for the house, like a new vacuum, unless she asked for it by name.

## What does mom actually want for Christmas?

<!-- visual: table | 6 habits matched to a gift, why it lands and skip it if -->

Usually something for herself, not the household. Comfort, rest and a reason to feel close to the family are the safe bets.

Look at where she spends her quiet time. The couch, the bed, the kitchen table with a cup of tea, or the phone calls with grandkids. The best gift makes one of those moments better.

| If mom... | Give her | Why it lands | Skip it if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Misses the grandkids | Wi-Fi photo frame | New photos arrive on their own | No Wi-Fi at home |
| Naps on the couch | Weighted blanket | Even, cozy weight | She sleeps hot (pick a knit one) |
| Reads every night | E-reader | Holds a whole library | She loves paper books |
| Lets her tea go cold | Heated mug | Holds her temperature | She drinks it fast |
| Loves a night in | Silk pillowcase or plush robe | Comfort she won't buy herself | She has a favorite already |
| Loves to cook | Enameled Dutch oven | Used all winter | Her cupboards are full |

## Which photo frame is easiest for mom?

Skylight for a mom who doesn't do apps, and Aura for a family that lives on their phones. Both let several people send photos to one frame.

PhotoWorkout says Skylight frames get photos by email, with no app needed by the sender. NBC Select says Aura runs through its app, with free unlimited cloud storage, and preferred the Aura overall. Load 20 or 30 photos before Christmas morning, and write the frame's email address on the card for the rest of the family. Our [photo frame comparison](/guides/aura-vs-skylight-vs-nixplay-photo-frame/) goes through all three.

## Is a weighted blanket a good gift for mom?

<!-- visual: chart | 10% rule: a 140 lb mom starts near 14 lb -->

For a mom who loves a nap or a movie on the couch, yes. Pick about 10% of her body weight, the rule of thumb Sleep Foundation gives.

If she runs warm, choose a knit one. Mattress Clarity calls Bearaby's Tree Napper incredibly breathable, and the whole blanket is machine washable. If she's always cold, Mattress Clarity says the beaded Gravity Blanket suits people who chill quickly. Puffy says people with breathing, joint or circulation problems should go lighter and check with a doctor first. Our [weighted blanket comparison](/guides/bearaby-vs-gravity-vs-ynm-weighted-blanket/) covers who each suits.

## What about the mom who reads?

<!-- visual: chart | E-reader weight: Kobo 174 g, Kindle 214 g; Kindle up to 12 weeks claimed -->

An e-reader if she reads a lot, and a book light if she sticks to paper. Both make reading in bed easier without keeping anyone else awake.

The Kindle Paperwhite is the pick if she already buys Kindle books. Amazon claims up to 12 weeks on a charge at half an hour a day, as iFixit reports. The Kobo Clara BW is lighter, 174 grams to the Kindle's 214 per comparisontabl.es, and has library borrowing built in. Our [e-reader comparison](/guides/kindle-paperwhite-vs-kobo-clara-bw/) has the full table.

## What's a small gift for a stocking?

Something she'll use up or reach for daily. A loose-leaf tea sampler, a box of artisan chocolates, a scented soy candle or a pair of cozy slippers all fit.

Slippers are a Christmas morning classic, and they get worn every day after. A tea sampler pairs well with a heated mug if you want a bigger gift, too.

## What should you avoid giving mom?

<!-- visual: callout | 4 gifts to skip unless she asked -->

Anything that's really a chore with a bow on it. Unless she asked for it, skip the household upgrade.

- **Appliances for the house:** a vacuum or a mop says "clean more." Give it only if it's on her list.
- **Clothing in a guessed size:** returns are a chore too. Pick a robe or slippers with easy sizing.
- **A weighted blanket that's too heavy:** stay near 10% of her body weight, and go lighter if unsure.
- **Gadgets she has to learn alone:** if it needs an app, set it up with her on Christmas Day.

## So what would I give?

A photo frame for a mom who misses the family, and a weighted blanket for a mom who loves the couch. Both get used every day through the winter, long after the wrapping is gone.

For a smaller gift, a heated mug and a tea sampler make a set. Add a note from the grandkids if there are any. It's often the part she keeps.
