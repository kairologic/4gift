For a reader on your list, whether it's a teen, a commuter or a mom with a stack by the bed, the Kindle Paperwhite and the Kobo Clara BW are the two e-readers most people choose between. They share a lot, so the gift comes down to where the books come from:

- **Kindle Paperwhite** for someone who already buys Kindle books or uses Audible. It has the bigger screen, and Amazon claims up to 12 weeks on a charge.
- **Kobo Clara BW** for the library borrower and the person who wants no ads. Libby borrowing is built in, and myereader notes the Clara BW has no ads by default.
- **Either one** for a reader by the pool or in the bath. Both are rated IPX8, good for 2 meters of fresh water for 60 minutes.
- **Skip both** for someone who mostly reads comics or color magazines. These are black-and-white screens.

## Where do their books come from now?

<!-- visual: table | Screen 7 vs 6 in, 300 ppi, weight 214 vs 174 g, library, ads and skip it if -->

Ask that first. It's the one thing that decides this gift.

If they have years of Kindle purchases, a Kindle keeps them all in one place. If they borrow from the public library, the Kobo makes that easier. Today.com says books borrowed on a Kobo download straight to the device, while on a Kindle you send each library book from the Libby app. Comparisontabl.es notes Libby's send-to-Kindle works in the US only.

## Is the bigger screen worth it?

<!-- visual: chart | Weight: Kindle 214 g, Kobo 174 g -->

For a lot of readers, yes. The Paperwhite's screen is about 7 inches against the Clara BW's 6, and the-ebook-reader says it looks and feels a bit nicer with its flush front.

The same reviewer notes it's a little more prone to glare and fingerprints. And the smaller Kobo is lighter, 174 grams against 214, which matters to someone who reads lying down or carries it everywhere. Today.com named the Clara BW its pick for travel.

Both screens are sharp at 300 pixels per inch. Comparisontabl.es lists the same E Ink Carta 1300 panel for both.

## What about ads on the lock screen?

The Kobo has none by default. The Kindle comes with or without ads, and the version without them costs more.

That's worth deciding before you buy. iFixit lists the Paperwhite as available with or without ads, and myereader says Amazon charges a fee for the no-ads version. If the recipient would be bothered by an ad on the sleep screen, either pick the ad-free Kindle or give the Kobo.

## Which one lasts longer on a charge?

<!-- visual: chart | Battery: Kindle up to 12 weeks claimed, 1900 vs 1500 mAh -->

Amazon makes the bigger claim. iFixit, quoting Amazon, says a single charge lasts up to 12 weeks at half an hour of reading a day with wireless off and the light at 13.

We didn't find a stated battery life for the Clara BW in the sources we read. Comparisontabl.es lists its battery at 1500 mAh against the Kindle's 1900 mAh. Both charge over USB-C, so either one shares a cable with most new phones.

## Can they listen to audiobooks?

<!-- visual: callout | Kindle Unlimited: up to 20 titles at a time -->

Both play audiobooks over Bluetooth, but the stores differ. Kindle ties into Audible. Today.com notes that on a Kobo, audiobooks borrowed from your library app won't play, so a library audiobook fan would still need their phone.

Subscriptions differ too. Kindle Unlimited lets members borrow up to 20 titles at a time, per myereader, and Kobo Plus is Kobo's own subscription for e-books and audiobooks. Check which one the recipient already pays for.

## Which is better for a student?

The Kindle, by a little. Myereader says Kindles are better study tools, mainly because highlights are easier to get off the device.

Today.com came down on the Kindle overall too, calling the Paperwhite the more reliable choice. For a teen who reads for school and fun, that's a good reason to pick it.

## So which e-reader would I give?

The Kindle Paperwhite for most readers, and the Kobo Clara BW for anyone who lives on library books.

The Paperwhite has the bigger screen, the longer stated battery life and the store most people already use. The Clara BW is lighter, has no ads, and puts the library one tap away. Either way, add a gift card for books, since a new e-reader with nothing on it is only half the gift.
