For the coffee drinker who reheats the same cup three times before noon, a heated mug is a gift that fixes a daily annoyance. The three best-known ones work in different ways, so think about where the recipient drinks:

- **Ember Mug 2** for the person who wants their exact temperature. You set it from 120 to 145°F in the Ember app, and it holds heat for up to 1.5 hours off its coaster.
- **Nextmug** for the person who wants a bigger mug and no app. It holds 14 ounces and has three heat presets on a button.
- **OHOM Ui** for the desk worker who leaves the mug in one place. It's a ceramic mug on a wireless warming pad, with no battery in the mug.
- **Skip all three** for someone who needs a cup to carry out the door. None of these is a travel mug.

## Do they want an app or a button?

<!-- visual: table | Size 10 to 15 oz, control, time off base, washing and skip it if for all 3 -->

That's the first split. The Ember Mug 2 is run from an app, and the other two aren't.

Best Buy's listing says you set the Ember anywhere from 120°F to 145°F in the Ember app, on iOS or Android. Without the app, idrinkcoffee lists a preset of 135°F. That's ideal for someone particular about temperature, and a chore for someone who hates another app on their phone.

The Nextmug has no Bluetooth and no app. AOL's reviewer describes a single power button that cycles through warm, hot and piping. The OHOM Ui has no controls to fuss with at all: Bob Vila says it heats to 130°F every time.

## How long does it stay hot away from the base?

<!-- visual: chart | Time off base: Ember up to 1.5 hours, Nextmug 40 minutes to 1.4 hours, OHOM Ui 0 -->

Ember goes longest. Best Buy says the Mug 2 holds temperature for up to 1.5 hours off its Charging Coaster 2, and all day on it.

Nextmug's time depends on the setting. AOL's reviewer reports up to 40 minutes on piping, 1.2 hours on hot and 1.4 hours on warm. The OHOM Ui keeps nothing warm off its pad, because there's no battery in the mug. Coolmomtech's reviewer says the heat comes from the ceramic mug working with the Qi charger, which acts like a warmer.

## What are the three heat settings on a Nextmug?

<!-- visual: table | Nextmug presets: AOL 135, 140, 145°F vs HGTV and Bob Vila 130, 140, 150°F -->

Warm, hot and piping, and the sources disagree on the exact numbers. AOL's 2023 review lists 135, 140 and 145°F. HGTV and Bob Vila list 130, 140 and 150°F.

Either way, it's three choices on one button. For many people, that's plenty.

## Which one is easiest to clean?

<!-- visual: callout | Washing: 1 of 3 dishwasher safe, Ember IPX7 to 1 meter -->

The OHOM Ui. Bob Vila lists the Ui Artist Collection mug as dishwasher safe.

The other two have electronics in the mug. Idrinkcoffee says the Ember Mug 2 is hand wash only, though it's rated IPX7, so it can be submerged up to 1 meter. Bob Vila says the Nextmug must be hand-washed and its lid can go in the dishwasher.

For a person who loads every dish into the machine, the Ui is the safest gift. For anyone else, a quick rinse is the routine.

## Which is best for an office?

HGTV named the OHOM Ui Plus its pick for the office. It sits on its pad on the desk, and there's no battery to charge or app to pair at work.

Coolmomtech's reviewer is honest about the trade-off: you can't walk around the office holding hot coffee all day. For a coworker who meets at a desk and stays there, that doesn't matter.

The Ember is the better office gift for someone who moves between meetings, since it keeps heat for up to 1.5 hours away from the coaster.

## What comes in the box?

Each one comes with its own charging base, so there's nothing extra to buy.

- **Ember Mug 2:** the mug and its Charging Coaster 2, which Best Buy says is required to charge.
- **Nextmug:** the mug and a coaster that serves as its charging dock, per AOL.
- **OHOM Ui:** coolmomtech lists the ceramic mug, the Qi charger, the cord and plug, and a fitted lid.

Best Buy says the Ember Mug 2 isn't microwave safe, so tell the recipient to warm the drink first and let the mug keep it there.

## So which mug would I give?

The Ember Mug 2 for the coffee lover who cares about the exact temperature, and the OHOM Ui for the coworker who never leaves their desk.

Ember tops the roundups: HGTV and Bob Vila both name it best overall, and it's the only one with an app and a stated hold time of 1.5 hours. The Nextmug is the pick for someone who wants 14 ounces and three simple settings. The OHOM Ui is the easiest to live with, as long as it stays on its pad.
